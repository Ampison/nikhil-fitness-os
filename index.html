Questions before you message

Straight answers. No over-selling.

Why should I trust you if you are not certified yet?

I am clear about that upfront. I do not replace a doctor or dietitian. What I offer is practical structure from a lifestyle I actually live, with proof previews of how I plan training and food.

Can I start from home?

Yes. My base was built from home training with limited equipment. If you have a gym, the plan can shift toward gym structure. If not, we build around what you have.

Do I need expensive food?

No. The system is built around normal Nepali foods: dal-bhat, eggs, chana, curd, chicken when available, vegetables, rice control, and simple portion rules.

What should I send on WhatsApp?

Send age, height, weight, goal, current food routine, home or gym access, photos if you are comfortable, and your biggest problem with consistency.
